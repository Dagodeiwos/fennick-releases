Fennick's Beast Core

Required Version 1.8.0

Works on: Forever ✅ · Classic HC 🛠 Compatibility

One window, one waypoint arrow, shared settings and a combat-safe monster tooltip for every module.

Features

  • Focus view: pick one module or Auto; only one module drives the arrow at a time
  • Monster tooltip: level range, elite or rare, quests on your route and notable drops, up to 8 lines
  • Teal-and-gold look, plus dark and minimal styles and colour-blind colours
  • Follows your UI scale and text size; matches Blizzard or ElvUI sizing
  • Move frames with Edit Mode or /beast move; no sounds unless you turn them on
