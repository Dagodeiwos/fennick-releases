Market Advisor Coming soon

Built for WoW Forever. A first download is not ready yet, and I am not setting a release date.

I am building Market Advisor as one complete suite, so you do not have to stitch together a stack of auction add-ons. It is meant to assess the live auction house and historical prices, then help you buy smarter, post smarter and keep the advantage.

Every number that can be measured belongs in the advice: what professions and quests actually need, what a vendor would pay, the discounts you get from reputation, and the deposit and auction-house cut that come off the top. The aim is simple: maximize your purchasing and selling power.

  • Fair-value buy and sell advice, applied wherever you look at a price
  • Price-outlier protection, so one wild listing does not distort the picture
  • A trend graph for the single item you have selected
  • Demand from profession recipes and quests
  • Vendor price floors, so you can see what a vendor would pay
  • Reputation discounts factored into the real cost
  • Deposit and auction-house cut math on every post
  • Posting and deal searching in one suite
  • A sales ledger of what you actually sold
  • Thorough window customization, so the suite can look and feel the way you want

Market Advisor will be released under the MIT License: free to use and share. Donations are optional and unlock nothing.
