  1. Download the install-all bundle, or single add-ons from all releases.
  2. Unzip it into your game's Interface/AddOns/ folder, so each Fennick_... folder sits directly inside AddOns.
  3. Restart the game and check the Fennick add-ons are enabled in the AddOns list at character select.

Fennick's Beast Core Required 1.8.0

Works on: Forever ✅ · Classic HC 🛠 Compatibility

One window, one waypoint arrow, shared settings and a combat-safe monster tooltip for every module.

  • Focus view: pick one module or Auto; only one module drives the arrow at a time
  • Monster tooltip: level range, elite or rare, quests on your route and notable drops, up to 8 lines
  • Teal-and-gold look, plus dark and minimal styles and colour-blind colours
  • Follows your UI scale and text size; matches Blizzard or ElvUI sizing
  • Move frames with Edit Mode or /beast move; no sounds unless you turn them on

More about Fennick's Beast Core

Fennick's Guidepost Optional 4.7.0

Works on: Forever ✅ · Classic HC 🛠 Compatibility

A stand-alone 1-60 leveling guide for every race and class, with PvE, Hardcore and PvP rulesets. No RXPGuides needed.

  • The top line is the one thing to do next: a verb, a target and a location
  • Race-first routes; class trainer and class quest steps only for your class
  • PvE (fastest), HC (no death routing, safe transport) and PvP rulesets
  • Steps complete from your quest log, never just from reaching a waypoint
  • Short first-run setup; profession hints only for professions you trained

More about Fennick's Guidepost

Fennick Rest Optional 1.2.4

Works on: Forever ✅ · Classic HC ⛔ Compatibility

Your rested XP, how long until it's full, and the best friendly place to log out.

  • Rested now, your cap and the time until full, inside or outside an inn
  • Hearth ready? It says hearth to your inn and log out
  • On cooldown, it names the nearest friendly inn or capital
  • Cozy Sleeping Bag and Camp Tent reminders when they apply

More about Fennick Rest

Fennick Reputation Optional 1.2.3

Works on: Forever ✅ · Classic HC 🛠 Compatibility

Shows the reputation turn-ins you can hand in right now with what's already in your bags.

  • City cloth donations, Timbermaw, Argent Dawn, Thorium Brotherhood and Cenarion Circle
  • Darkmoon Faire turn-ins only in the faire's week and zone
  • Checks your faction side before suggesting anything

More about Fennick Reputation

Fennick Merchant's Favor Optional 1.2.3

Works on: Forever ✅ · Classic HC ⛔ Compatibility

Tracks the Forever Merchant's Favor crates, writs and labels in your bags from start to turn-in.

  • Waylaid crates, Craftsman's Writs and Shipping Labels, several at once, sorted by readiness
  • Live objectives with have/need and bag counts
  • Writ customers' names and map spots, marked unconfirmed until checked in game
  • Steps finish only when the game reports the turn-in

More about Fennick Merchant's Favor

Fennick's Trailmarks Optional 1.1.2

Works on: Forever ✅ · Classic HC 🛠 Compatibility

Remembers where you found ore, herbs, chests, quest targets and quest givers, and marks them on your world map and minimap. Busy spots show as shaded areas.

  • Records ore and herb nodes, treasure chests, quest targets and quest givers
  • 13 ore and 28 herb kinds, each with its own pin and filter
  • Places with 6 or more finds show as shaded areas
  • Per-type toggles, pin size and area opacity
  • Share my finds as read-only copy text

More about Fennick's Trailmarks

Fennick's Cartographer Optional 1.2.0

Works on: Forever ✅ · Classic HC 🛠 Compatibility

Your map key opens a detailed world map with travel routes and a "how do I get there?" trip planner, plus a square minimap, icon filters for each map and your own pins.

  • World map in the game's own map art with explored areas: drag to pan, wheel to zoom, click a zone to open it, coordinates, party and pin search
  • Flight paths, ships, zeppelins and the Deeprun Tram as route lines; Shift-click a spot for a trip using only the flight points you know, with minutes per leg
  • Icon filters for the world map and minimap separately: quests, flight masters, innkeepers, vendors, trainers, ore, herbs, chests and every Trailmarks type
  • Your own pins with a name, a note and any icon; share them as copy-only text
  • Square minimap by default (round is a setting) with the Fennick border, zoom, coordinates and 16 px pins
  • Shows Trailmarks, Guidepost, Rest, Reputation and Merchant's Favor pins; turn it off and Blizzard's map comes straight back
  • Never changes anything during combat

Bugs and help

Open a bug report on GitHub issues. Include the add-on, version, client language and the steps. To capture errors, type /console scriptErrors 1 in chat, reproduce the problem and paste the error text. /fennick debuglog gives a log you can paste too.
