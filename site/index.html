Fennick's Addons

Free add-ons for WoW Forever. Fennick's Guidepost is a stand-alone 1-60 leveling guide for every race and class, with PvE, Hardcore and PvP rulesets, plus helpers for rested XP, reputation and Merchant's Favor.

Free and always will be. One step at a time, out of your way.

Download

Download the install-all bundle

Core 1.5.1, Guidepost 4.5.2, Rest 1.2.3, Reputation 1.2.2, Merchant's Favor 1.2.2, Trailmarks 1.0.2, Cartographer 1.0.1. Zip, 1.5 MB. Released Oct 27, 2026.

  1. Download the install-all bundle above, or single add-ons from all releases.
  2. Unzip it into your game's Interface/AddOns/ folder, so each Fennick_... folder sits directly inside AddOns.
  3. Restart the game and check the Fennick add-ons are enabled in the AddOns list at character select.

Fennick's Beast Core is required by every module. Add any mix of the others.

  • Fennick's Beast Core Required 1.5.0
    One window, one waypoint arrow, shared settings and a combat-safe monster tooltip for every module.
  • Fennick's Guidepost Optional 4.5.2
    A stand-alone 1-60 leveling guide for every race and class, with PvE, Hardcore and PvP rulesets. No RXPGuides needed.
  • Fennick Rest Optional 1.2.3
    Your rested XP, how long until it's full, and the best friendly place to log out.
  • Fennick Reputation Optional 1.2.2
    Shows the reputation turn-ins you can hand in right now with what's already in your bags.
  • Fennick Merchant's Favor Optional 1.2.2
    Tracks the Forever Merchant's Favor crates, writs and labels in your bags from start to turn-in.
  • Fennick's Trailmarks Optional 1.0.0
    Remembers where you found ore, herbs, chests, quest targets and quest givers, and marks them on your world map and minimap. Busy spots show as shaded areas.
  • Fennick's Cartographer Optional 1.0.0
    A detailed world map and minimap with zoom and pan, flight paths, ships, zeppelins and the tram, a "how do I get there?" trip planner, icon filters for each map and your own pins with any icon.

For playtesters: Fennick's Fox Trials is an optional in-game checklist for people helping test Fennick, with results you can copy as text. It's a separate download on the releases page; regular players don't need it.

Bugs and help

Email fennick@fennickaddons.dev or open a bug report. Include the add-on, version, class and level, and the steps. In game, /fennick help shows the address and /fennick debuglog gives a log you can paste.
